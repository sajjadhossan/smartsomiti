# PAYMENT_COLLECTION.md — Member Savings & Loan Collections

## 1. Collection Types
- **Regular Savings Deposit**: Credited to `Account 2110 Member Savings Deposits`.
- **Loan Principal Repayment**: Credited to `Account 1141 Loan Principal Receivable`.
- **Loan Interest Income**: Credited to `Account 4110 Loan Interest Income`.
- **Membership & Admission Fees**: Credited to `Account 4210 Admission Fees`.

## 2. Field Officer Mobile Collections
- Field officers enter collection amounts into the mobile offline draft cache.
- Two-step maker-checker: Collector submits -> Branch Cashier verifies physical cash deposit -> System posts to General Ledger and issues digital SMS money receipt.
