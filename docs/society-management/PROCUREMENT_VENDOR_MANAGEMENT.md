# PROCUREMENT_VENDOR_MANAGEMENT.md — Procurement, Vendor Management & Purchase Orders

## 1. Procurement Lifecycle
1. **Purchase Request (PR)**: Staff or branch submits requirement.
2. **Workflow Approval**: Manager / Executive Committee verifies budget and approves PR.
3. **Purchase Order (PO)**: PO issued to verified vendor with quotation reference.
4. **Goods Receipt Note (GRN)**: Storekeeper receives physical items and updates inventory balance.
5. **Bill Verification & Payment**: Accounts verifies bill against GRN and executes payment.

## 2. Vendor Registry
Maintains vendor profiles, bank details, tax/BIN information, performance rating, and purchase history.
