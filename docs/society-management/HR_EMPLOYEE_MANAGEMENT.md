# HR_EMPLOYEE_MANAGEMENT.md — Employee & HR Management System

## 1. HR Architecture
Supports structured staff and management governance within individual cooperative societies:
- **Departments**: Field Operations, Accounts & Finance, Loan Recovery, Administration, IT Support.
- **Designations**: Managing Director, Branch Manager, Senior Field Officer, Cashier, Recovery Officer.
- **Employee Record**: Full name, contact, joining date, reporting manager, base salary structure, emergency contact, active status.

## 2. Onboarding Workflow
1. Employee registration & profile creation.
2. Mandatory document upload (CV, NID, academic certificates, police clearance).
3. HR verification & administrative review.
4. Executive approval via Phase 3 Workflow Engine.
5. Role & system access provisioning (RBAC). Newly onboarded staff start with minimal privileges by default.
