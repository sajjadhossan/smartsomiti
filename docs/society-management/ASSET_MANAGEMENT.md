# ASSET_MANAGEMENT.md — Fixed Asset Registry & Depreciation

## 1. Asset Distinction
Fixed assets are permanent society property (laptops, motorcycles for field recovery, furniture, cash safe, biometric devices) distinct from consumable inventory.

## 2. Asset Lifecycle
- `REQUESTED` -> `PURCHASED` -> `REGISTERED` -> `ASSIGNED` -> `IN_USE` -> `UNDER_MAINTENANCE` -> `RETIRED` -> `DISPOSED`.

## 3. Financial Integration
- Capitalization: `Debit Account 1210 Office Equipment / 1220 Furniture`, `Credit Account 1120 Bank`.
- Depreciation: Straight-line or Reducing-balance monthly depreciation schedule posted to `Account 5510 Depreciation Expense` and `Account 1290 Accumulated Depreciation`.
