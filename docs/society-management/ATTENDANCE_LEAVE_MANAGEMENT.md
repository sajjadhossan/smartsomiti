# ATTENDANCE_LEAVE_MANAGEMENT.md — Attendance & Leave Management Engine

## 1. Attendance Methods
- **Self Check-In / Check-Out**: Authorized staff daily punch with timestamp.
- **Administrator Entry**: Branch manager bulk entry or shift verification.
- **Correction Workflow**: Discrepancies (missed punch) require formal correction request and manager approval.

## 2. Leave Types & Policies
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: Annual quota (e.g. 10 days).
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: Annual quota (e.g. 14 days with medical prescription).
- **Earned Leave (EL)**: Accrued based on service tenure.
- **Maternity / Paternity Leave**: Statutory cooperative compliance.
- **Unpaid Leave (LWP)**: Impacts monthly payroll calculation automatically.

## 3. Workflow Integration
All leave requests route through the Phase 3 Approval Engine. Self-approval is strictly forbidden.
