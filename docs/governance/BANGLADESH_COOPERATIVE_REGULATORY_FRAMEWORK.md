# Bangladesh Co-operative Societies Act 2001 & Rules 2004 Regulatory Framework

## 1. Statutory Legal Basis

In Bangladesh, cooperative societies are governed by the **Co-operative Societies Act, 2001 (সমবায় সমিতি আইন, ২০০১)** and the **Co-operative Societies Rules, 2004 (সমবায় সমিতি বিধিমালা, ২০০৪)**, administered under the Ministry of Local Government, Rural Development and Co-operatives (পল্লী উন্নয়ন ও সমবায় বিভাগ) and the Department of Co-operatives (সমবায় অধিদপ্তর).

## 2. Core Statutory Compliance Requirements

### 2.1 Annual General Meeting (AGM) — Section 23
- **Statutory Deadline**: Every registered cooperative society must convene an Annual General Meeting within **9 months** of the conclusion of each financial year (by 31st March following June 30 year-end).
- **Mandatory Agenda**:
  1. Confirmation of minutes of the preceding AGM.
  2. Consideration and adoption of the annual working report of the Managing Committee.
  3. Presentation, consideration, and approval of audited annual financial balance sheets and Profit & Loss statements.
  4. Allocation of net surplus/profit in compliance with Section 34.
  5. Consideration of the statutory audit report issued by the Department of Co-operatives.
  6. Adoption of the annual budget for the succeeding financial year.
  7. Election of Managing Committee members upon expiration of term.
- **Notice Period**: Minimum **15 days** written notice before the meeting date (Rule 24).
- **Quorum**: Quorum threshold as specified in the society's registered by-laws (typically minimum 1/3rd or 1/5th of registered members).

### 2.2 Statutory Profit Allocations — Section 34
Before declaring any dividend or patronage refund to members, the society must mathematically allocate:
1. **Statutory Reserve Fund (সংবিধিবদ্ধ সংরক্ষিত তহবিল)**: Minimum **15%** of annual net profit.
2. **Co-operative Development Fund (সমবায় উন্নয়ন তহবিল)**: Mandatory **3%** of annual net profit payable to the national Cooperative Development Fund per Section 34(2).
3. **Bad Debt Reserve / Contingency**: Determined in consultation with the statutory auditor.

### 2.3 Managing Committee (ব্যবস্থাপনা কমিটি) Governance
- **Committee Term**: The tenure of an elected Managing Committee is **3 years** from the date of assuming office.
- **Election Procedure**: Elections must be announced at least **30 days** before the expiration of the current committee's term and conducted under a neutral Election Committee.
- **Disqualification Rules**: Members defaulting on loans or overdue deposits are disqualified from contesting or holding committee office.

### 2.4 Annual Statutory Audit — Section 37
- Financial accounts must be audited annually by the Registrar of Co-operative Societies or an auditor deputed by the Department of Co-operatives.
- Copy of the approved audit report must be submitted to the District Co-operative Officer (জেলা সমবায় অফিসার) within **30 days** of adoption.

## 3. SaaS Platform Enforcement Mechanisms

1. **Automated Statutory Reminders**: Asia/Dhaka timezone compliance engine issues alerts 90, 60, 30, and 15 days ahead of statutory filing deadlines.
2. **Reserve Fund Lock Invariant**: General Ledger closing algorithms forbid net profit distributions unless GL Accounts 3001 (Reserve Fund) and 3002 (Co-op Development Fund) have received their statutory 15% and 3% journal postings.
3. **Audit Trail Immutability**: All regulatory submissions, minutes, and resolution filings are versioned and signed with non-repudiation logging.
